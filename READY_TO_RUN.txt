MONEYPILOT 1.3.0 - READY TO RUN
===============================

Double-click "MoneyPilot" in this folder.

If the shortcut is unavailable, open the MoneyPilot folder and double-click
MoneyPilot.exe. No installation, Python, Flutter, Node.js, API key, or internet
connection is required.

FIRST LAUNCH
1. Choose "Create an account".
2. Enter your name, email, and a strong password.
3. Save the one-time recovery code somewhere private.
4. Open the empty workspace and add only your real records when ready.

The Local Coach works offline. It answers from the records you enter and will say
when information is missing. Suggested budget changes require your approval.

LANGUAGE
Open Settings > Appearance & language to select English, French, or Arabic.
Arabic automatically switches the app to right-to-left layout. The Coach
understands and replies in the selected language.

CALENDAR AND THEMES
Open Calendar to review daily income, expenses, transactions, no-spend days,
and the month's highest-spending day. Settings includes Ocean, Cyan, Forest,
Violet, and Sunset palettes plus optional glow effects.

Use Settings to sign out, securely wipe financial data, change coaching
preferences, or permanently delete a local profile. Wiping requires the word
WIPE. Account deletion requires the account password. Financial records are
isolated per profile on this Windows device.
